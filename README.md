# Kilroy

Your memories. At the places they happened.

## What is Kilroy?

Kilroy surfaces photos and notes related to the global location where they happened. No filters, no remote uploading, just moments captured and anchored toe places.

## How it works

1. Grant location and photos permissions
2. Live your life
3. If there is an image or note in your physical location (that you're authenticated to see), Kilroy taps you to let you know. 
4. Leave a photo memory or note where they happened IRL, date- and time-stamped. Move on. Grant permissions to family and friends for generations to come.
5. Unlock messages from yourself in the past, future friends, and like-minded creatures exploring this world right here and now.

## Technical

- SwiftUI + iOS 17+
- CoreLocation for location tracking
- iPhone altimeter for height correlation, in multi-floor buildings
- Photos framework for spatial indexing
- CoreHaptics for tactile feedback
- Apple,Google, Meta APIs & SDKs for login, maps, social permissions.
